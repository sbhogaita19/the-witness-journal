If practice brings panic, disorientation, loss of sleep, or makes ordinary life hard, stop. Feel your feet, name five things you can see, speak to someone you trust, and seek qualified help if it continues. This journal is not medical care.

Keep the traditions distinct.

“Witness” and “seer” can be useful pointers here, but Buddhist no-self does not simply mean a permanent inner witness. Privacy

Your writing stays in this browser

Entries and voice notes are stored locally on this device using your browser’s site storage. There is no account, cloud sync, analytics or hidden upload - recordings are never sent anywhere. Clearing Brave site data, uninstalling the app, or losing the device can erase them.

Back up regularly. The JSON backup includes your voice notes as embedded audio, so one file restores writing and voice together. A plain-text export is easier to read but cannot be re-imported. In rare cases a browser can free site storage under heavy pressure, so keep a recent backup.

Reset

Delete all journal entries

This removes every entry and voice note stored by this journal on this device. Export first if you may want them later.

About the enquiry

No promise, diagnosis or spiritual score

The journal cannot produce, verify or grade an “awareness state”. It offers prompts for direct observation. Spiritual language here remains open to examination, and no entry is a diagnosis.
